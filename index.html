Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions.

What website do you use to stream snipe?

The community uses StreamScope.

When are events hosted?

Events are scheduled and are mostly hosted on Saturdays and Sundays. Check #team-assembly for upcoming events and updates.

How can I become a moderator?

Stay active in chat, help other members, and be a positive part of the community. Moderators are handpicked by Dimchig.

Can I get banned from participating?

Follow the community rules and Epic Games' rules while participating. No one can guarantee how a platform will handle rule violations, so play fairly and keep it respectful.
